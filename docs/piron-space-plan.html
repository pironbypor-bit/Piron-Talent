Piron Work Space

The owner control room. Step by step build plan. Written 15 October 2026.

Goal. One private place where only you see: how many users, what is broken, which new features are still hidden, and how much money came in and went out. Everything is read-only first, then action buttons are added one by one.

Step 0. Decisions (defaults I will use unless you change them)

QuestionDefault
Who can open it?Only your account (owner). Other admins keep the current queues but not the Space.
How are errors collected?My own error table in the database (free). Sentry can be added later.
How do you earn?Not decided. The finance screen takes any income type you type in (fee, subscription, listing, other).
How do alerts reach you?In the app (bell) and by email through Brevo.
Where does it live?Under /admin, as a new front page with a side list of sections.

The steps

Step 1Space shell and Overview
  • You get: a front page with today's numbers, "needs you now" counts (identity checks, reports, payout reviews, waiting requests), and a system status strip (app, database, email, push, scheduled jobs).
  • Build: owner flag on the admin record (migration 0045), audit log table, one function that returns the overview numbers, the new page and side list, links to the existing queues.
  • Demo first: yes, one screen with sample numbers.
  • Checks: a non-owner cannot read the numbers; every owner action writes an audit line; rule test 07.
Step 2People
  • You get: total users, new this week and month, providers, identity checked, active in the last 7 days, growth by day, signups by city, a searchable people list with each person's jobs and reports.
  • Build: people summary and growth functions, people list page with search, person detail page. Suspend and unsuspend buttons come last in this step.
  • Demo first: no, build on the real numbers.
  • Checks: no email, phone or ID number is shown; suspend is logged; rule test 08.
Step 3Marketplace health
  • You get: the funnel (posts, quotes, accepted, delivered, confirmed), time to first quote, cancel rate, average stars, jobs that look stuck, busiest and emptiest categories and cities.
  • Build: one function per block, a page with simple bars (no chart library needed), a stuck jobs list with a link to open each job.
  • Checks: numbers match a hand count on the test data.
Step 4Errors
  • You get: a list of what failed: time, page, action, message, how many times, last seen. A red badge on Overview when something repeats. Mark an error as fixed or ignored.
  • Build: error table (migration 0046), a small logger used by server actions and a catch-all for page errors, grouping of repeats, an email alert through Brevo when one error repeats 5 times in 10 minutes, a list page and detail page, automatic clean up after 60 days.
  • Also shows: background job results (emails sent or failed, push sent or failed, reminders ran).
  • Checks: a person's private text is never stored in an error message; a made-up error appears, groups and alerts; rule test 09.
Step 5Feature switches and change log
  • You get: a list of features with a switch each: off, only me, chosen people, everyone. You can try a new feature live before anyone else sees it. A log of what was turned on, by whom, and when.
  • Build: feature table and one check function (migration 0047), a helper the app uses to hide or show a feature, the switches page, and wrapping the first hidden feature. The held payments idea stays a demo only.
  • Checks: a hidden feature cannot be opened by typing its address; switching is logged; rule test 10.
Step 6Money
  • You get: revenue entries (date, type, amount, note), cost entries (Cloudflare, Supabase, Brevo, domains, other, with monthly repeat), a monthly view with revenue, costs and profit, and a market volume number: the total of invoices users marked as paid (market size only, not your income).
  • Build: finance tables, owner only (migration 0048), entry forms, monthly report page, CSV download.
  • Demo first: yes, the monthly report with sample numbers.
  • Important: Piron Work does not touch user money. This is your own books. When a payment partner exists later, income can be filled in automatically.
  • Checks: only the owner can read or write; totals match a hand calculation; rule test 11.
Step 7Reports, exports and audit view
  • You get: a one page monthly owner report (users, jobs, money, errors) with a print button, CSV downloads for people, jobs, invoices and finance, and a page that shows the audit log.
  • Build: report page, export routes, audit list with a filter.
  • Checks: exports exclude private fields; only the owner can download.
Step 8Finish
  • Full test run: code, database rules, live pages as owner and as a normal user.
  • Security review of the new migrations, same as the last one.
  • Update the handover and CLAUDE.md. The Space is English only at first; Khmer lines can follow.

What I need from you, and when

  • Before step 1: say OK, or change the defaults in step 0.
  • Before step 4: nothing, unless you prefer Sentry (then you create the account and paste the key).
  • Before step 6: tell me how you plan to earn, so the income types match.
  • After each step: open the page as owner and tell me what feels wrong.

What stays out for now

  • Real payments through Piron Work (locked until legal advice).
  • Staff roles with limited views (can be added after step 7).
  • Product analytics tools and page view tracking.
