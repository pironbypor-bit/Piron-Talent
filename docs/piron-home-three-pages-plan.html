Home in three pages

Plan and features. Written 16 October 2026. Nothing is built yet.

The idea. Today Home is one long list of posts. It becomes three pages, chosen with three buttons at the top of Home. Each page answers one question.

Page 1. Posts

Same as today, with the three-button strip above it.

  • Search bar, kind filter (Job offer, Skill offer, Share knowledge) and the Post button stay.
  • The filter and search remember what you chose when you come back.
  • The “Get started” card stays on Profile.

Page 2. Professionals (the catalogue)

What a card shows

  • Picture: the provider’s photo for that service, or a friendly category icon when there is none.
  • Name: a person or a company, with the green Identity checked mark and an Available now dot.
  • Services line: up to three things they do (“Home wiring · Lighting · Fans”).
  • Rating and count, and the city.
  • Price from with the unit (per hour, day, session or project).
  • Book button, and a small Keep (save) mark.

The page

  • Header “Available professionals” with the count (“6 professionals”).
  • Search by name, service or skill. Filters: category, city or province, price range, rating (4+ stars), remote, available now, person or company.
  • Sort: best rated, lowest price, most completed jobs, newest.
  • Cards two per row on a phone, three on a wide screen. A “Show more” button loads the next ones.
  • Tapping the card opens the full profile (record, reviews, portfolio, all their services). Tapping Book starts a booking.

How the profile and this page connect

The profile says who you are (name, picture, record, reviews, identity). A service card says what you offer (title, price from, city). People appear on the Professionals page in two ways at once:

WayWhat happens
Automatic first cardWhen someone turns on I offer a service on their profile, a first card is made from the profile: name, picture, role line, category, skills, price from, city, rating. No extra typing. It appears as soon as the profile has a role line, a category, a city and a price from.
Their own cards (My services)They can add more cards, or replace the automatic one, each with its own title, photo, price and hours. Example: an electrician with “Home wiring” and “Solar panel check”.
  • The switch I offer a service on the profile is the master on/off. Off, or a paused account, hides every card.
  • A card always links to the profile for the record and reviews. Reviews and the record belong to the person, not to one card.
  • A profile that is too empty does not appear. The owner sees a small checklist: “Add a role line, category, city and price from to appear.”
  • Companies work the same way: turn on Offer services on the company page and a card is made from the company details; the owner or a manager can add more.

What providers and companies need

The catalogue is only as good as the service cards in it, so providers get a small My services screen under Profile → I offer a service:

  • Add a service: title, category, short description, price from and unit, city, remote yes or no, photo, days and hours they work.
  • Several services per person. Pause or delete any of them.
  • A company can list services too, from its own page, with a manager or owner as the contact.
  • A paused account or an account with no services does not appear.

Book: what happens when someone taps it

  1. Customer chooses a date and time, writes what they need, adds the place if it is on site, and sees the “from” price. Presses Send booking request.
  2. Provider gets a notification and has three choices: Accept at the shown price, Suggest another price or time, or Decline with a short reason.
  3. If the provider suggests a change, the customer accepts or declines it. Nothing is final until both agree.
  4. Once agreed it becomes a normal job with the same steps as today: provider marks delivered, customer confirms, review, invoice record, dispute if needed.
  5. A request that nobody answers in 48 hours is closed and the customer is told.
Money. Unchanged. Booking is a request and an agreement. Piron Work does not take or hold money; the customer pays the provider directly and the app keeps the record.

Why reuse the job steps: delivery, confirmation, reviews, invoices, disputes, reminders and the owner’s numbers already work. A booking only adds the front door.

Page 3. My bookings and applications

  • Filter buttons: All, Bookings, Applications. A second row: Waiting, Active, Done, Cancelled.
  • Two roles in one list: things you booked (as customer) and requests you received (as provider), each marked with a small label.
  • Each row: title, the other person or company, date, a coloured status, and the next action (Open, Cancel, Accept, Withdraw, Review).
  • Applications: job applications you sent from the Jobs tab with their status: Submitted, Shortlisted, Accepted, Rejected, Withdrawn.
  • Also shown, so nothing is lost: quotes you sent and projects you accepted appear as “Quote” rows that open the project.
  • Empty state like your picture: “No bookings or applications yet” with Explore professionals.
  • Provider shortcut: a count on the page button when requests are waiting for your answer.

Rules and safety

RuleWhy
You cannot book yourself or your own company.Prevents fake reviews and fake records.
A provider needs a complete profile to list a service. Identity check is shown but not required at first.Keeps the catalogue from being empty on day one; the green mark does the persuading.
Booking requests are limited per day per person.Stops spam, same as the message request limit.
Paused accounts cannot book or be booked.Same pause rules as the rest of the app.
Blocked people cannot book each other.Same block rules as messages.
Reviews only after a confirmed job.Same as today.

What changes in the rest of the app

  • Services tab: the new Professionals page replaces it, so there is one catalogue, not two. The bottom bar becomes Home, Payments, Jobs, Messages. Old Services addresses keep working and lead to Professionals.
  • Profile: the “I offer a service” card gets My services and a booking requests count.
  • Notifications: new kinds: booking requested, accepted, declined, changed, cancelled, expired. They join the Projects and quotes group, with Khmer.
  • Reminders: a nudge to the provider after 24 hours of no answer.
  • Piron Work Space: a feature switch for the whole booking feature (start Off, then Only me), and bookings added to the Marketplace funnel and monthly report.
  • Guides: both PDF guides updated at the end.
  • Fee (later): if you charge a service fee, bookings are counted the same as other confirmed jobs.
