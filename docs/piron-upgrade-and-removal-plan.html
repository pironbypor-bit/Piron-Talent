Upgrade and removal plan

Home in three pages, without duplicates. Written 16 October 2026. This replaces the build steps in piron-home-three-pages-plan.html; the card rules there (automatic, editable, unpublish) still stand.

One rule. Every thing in the app has exactly one home. Where a new page would repeat an old one, the old one is upgraded in place and the extra door is removed.

4. Build steps (each one can be switched off)

Step 3aUpgrade Services into Professionals.
  • Cards read the profile automatically; edited fields from the new card table (already created and tested) replace them.
  • Page: search, category, city, sort, Available now, Identity checked, Keep, the card grid.
  • Card needs a role line, a city and a price from to appear. Others see the “Needs details” checklist on their own card.
  • The button says View profile for now (Book arrives in step 4).
  • /app/services redirects here when the switch is on.
Rollback: switch Off and Services is the page it is today.
Step 3bMy card. One screen for the person: Published switch, name on the card, services line, price from, city, short description, Use profile details again, and a preview. Reached from Profile and from the Professionals page.
Step 3cRemove the Services tab from the bottom bar and fix every link in section 3. Old addresses redirect.
Step 4Book. Request, accept, suggest another price or time, decline, expire after 48 hours. When agreed it becomes a normal job. The button on cards changes from View profile to Book.
Step 5Upgrade My jobs and requests into Bookings. Same page, now also listing bookings and applications, with filters and next actions. /app/profile/jobs redirects to it; Profile rows link to it.
Step 6Notifications, 24-hour reminder, Khmer, owner numbers (bookings in the Marketplace funnel and the monthly report).
Step 7Test run and security review, update both PDF guides and the handover, then set the switch to Everyone when you say so.

5. Risks and how they are handled

RiskHandling
Someone has a saved link to Services or a provider listPermanent redirects, search words and filters carried over.
Existing providers vanish because their profile is thinBefore switching to Everyone I list who would be hidden and tell them; the profile page and the old public directory stay.
Two ways to hire confuse peoplePlain labels: Jobs (employers hiring workers), Post (a project, you get quotes), Book (choose a person now). Short explainers in both guides.
Fake bookings or spamDaily limit, no self-booking, blocked and paused people cannot book, reviews only after a confirmed job.
Removing the tab hurts visitors who are logged outThe public directory at /providers is not touched.

6. What stays the same for users

  • No new account, no new fees, no money moves through Piron Work.
  • Everything they can do today still works, from the same buttons or a clearly marked new one.
  • Until the switch is Everyone, only you see any of it.
