1 Finance & Bookkeeping

Providers are called: bookkeepers and accountants

Intro (one sentence under the title)

1.intro Monthly books, receipts, invoices and simple reports for shops, small businesses and freelancers.

Common tasks (buttons the visitor can press)

  1. 1.task 1 Monthly bookkeeping
  2. 1.task 2 Sorting receipts and expenses
  3. 1.task 3 Invoices and payment follow-up
  4. 1.task 4 Simple profit and loss report
  5. 1.task 5 Excel stock and sales sheets
  6. 1.task 6 Preparing records for a tax agent

What to prepare

  1. 1.prepare 1 Receipts, invoices and bank or wallet statements for the period
  2. 1.prepare 2 The accounting tool or Excel file you already use, if any
  3. 1.prepare 3 A rough count of your transactions in a normal month

How to choose

  1. 1.choose 1 Ask for a sample report or the template they use
  2. 1.choose 2 Agree what is included each month and the day you receive the books
  3. 1.choose 3 Share only the records needed, never your account passwords

Questions and answers

1.q1 How is the price set?

The provider quotes after reading your task. Monthly bookkeeping can be priced by the number of transactions, so ask what is included.

1.q2 Does a bookkeeper also file my tax?

Not always. Bookkeeping and tax filing are different jobs. Post tax filing as its own task or choose a provider in Tax Agency.
