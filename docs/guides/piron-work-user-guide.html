1. What Piron Work is

Piron Work is a place in Phnom Penh where people post work they need done, offer a skill, share what they know, and find volunteers. A customer and a provider agree on the work, the work is done and confirmed, and the provider builds a record of finished jobs that others can see.

Piron Work does not take, hold or move money. You and the other person agree the price and pay each other directly, in cash or by ABA, Wing or bank. The app only keeps records of what was agreed and what was paid.
  • Languages: Khmer and English. Switch with the ខ្មែរ / EN buttons at the top of the website.
  • No ads, no ranking: posts are shown newest first.

2. Getting started

Create an account

  1. Open app.piron.tech and choose Log in, then create an account.
  2. Enter your name, email and a password with at least 8 characters.
  3. Check your email and tap the confirmation link, then log in.
  4. Open Profile and follow the Get started card: complete your profile, start the identity check, write your first post.

Log in again

Use your email and password. If you forget the password, choose Reset password.

If you forgot your password

  1. Open Reset password and type your email. Press Send reset link.
  2. Open the newest email from Piron Work and tap the link once. It works in any browser or phone.
  3. Type a new password: at least 8 characters, different from your old one, not an easy word.
  4. Press Save new password. You are logged in.
Tip: every new request cancels the older email. If a link says it has expired, ask for one new link and use only that one.

Use it like a phone app

Open app.piron.tech in your phone browser and choose Add to home screen. Then open Settings inside the app and switch on phone notifications so you hear about quotes and messages.

4. Posting

Press the Post button on Home. You see four tiles:

TileUse it when
Post a job offerYou need something done. Add a title, what you need, an optional budget, deadline, deliverables and whether it can be done remotely.
Offer a skillYou are a provider and want people to find you.
Share what you knowYou want to help others with a tip or answer.
VolunteerYou want to find or organise volunteer activities (section 9).
  1. Choose a tile. The form opens with that kind chosen.
  2. Fill in the title and text. You can use the microphone button to speak instead of typing (on devices that allow it).
  3. Choose a category if it helps people find you.

Shortcut: on the form there are ready-made templates for common jobs such as product photos, menu design or logo refresh. Pick one and change anything.

5. Finding, booking and hiring

Three ways to hire

WayUse it whenWhat happens
Book (Professionals)You already know what kind of person you want.You send a booking request to one person. They accept, suggest another price or time, or decline.
Post a job offerYou want to compare offers.Several providers send quotes. You accept one.
Jobs tabAn employer wants workers for shifts or part-time work.People apply to the listing.

Find a professional

  1. Open Home and press Professionals. Search by name, role or skill.
  2. Narrow the list with the category, city and sort drop-downs, and the Available now, Identity checked and Remote buttons.
  3. Each card shows the picture, name, what they do, rating, city and the price from. Open a person to see their record, reviews, portfolio and the green Identity checked mark.
  4. Press Say hello to send one short message request, Invite to a project to ask them to quote on a post of yours, or Request a quote. Press Keep to save the provider for later.

Book a professional

  1. Press Book on a card or on the person’s page.
  2. Say what you need. Add a date and time, the place and your budget if you like. The price on the card is only a starting price.
  3. Press Send booking request. Under Bookings you see it as Waiting for the provider.
  4. The provider can Accept at their price, Suggest another price or time, or Decline. If they suggest a change, you see it under Needs your answer and press Accept the change or Decline.
  5. When you both agree, a normal job opens. Delivery, confirmation, review and invoice work exactly as below.
A request nobody answers is closed after 48 hours, and the person who must answer gets one reminder after 24 hours. You can press Cancel this request while it is open. You can send up to 5 requests a day and have one open request per person. You pay the provider directly; Piron Work does not take or hold money.

From request to finished job

  1. You post a job offer (or invite one provider you like).
  2. Providers send quotes with a price and number of days.
  3. You accept one quote with Accept quote. This opens the job and a chat with that provider.
  4. The provider delivers. They press Mark as delivered.
  5. You confirm. Press Confirm completion when you are happy. The job counts on the provider’s record.
  6. You rate the work. Choose stars, pick up to three skills that stood out, and press Send review.

A progress bar on the job shows the four steps: Quote accepted, Work delivered, Customer confirmed, Review left.

Hire again: on your finished job or the provider’s page, choose Hire again. The old scope is filled in and sent to that provider first. If they do not answer in 24 hours it opens to everyone, unless you choose to open it now.

If something goes wrong

Talk first in the job chat. If you cannot agree, you or the provider can press Open a dispute on the job; the Piron Work team reads both sides.

6. Doing the work

Your card in Professionals

When you turn on I offer a service on your profile, a card is made for you from the profile: name, picture, skills, price from, city and rating. A card appears only when it has what you do, a city and a price from.

  • Edit it: open Profile → My card (or Edit my card on the Professionals page). Change the name on the card, the services line, the price from, the city or the short description. A line you leave empty follows your profile.
  • Use profile details again removes your edits.
  • Unpublish: switch Published off to hide your card. Your profile, your record and jobs already agreed stay.
  • Answer requests: new booking requests are under Bookings → Needs your answer.

When you do a job

  1. Turn on Available this week on your Profile when you can take work. It lasts 7 days and shows others you are free.
  2. Press Send a quote on the post: price, delivery days and a short note.
  3. When your quote is accepted, use the job chat to agree the details.
  4. When finished, press Mark as delivered and tell the customer in the chat.
  5. After the customer confirms, create an invoice if you want a record (section 13).

Your record (finished jobs and reviews) is shown on your profile. You can hide it with Show my work record in Settings.

7. The Jobs tab

Companies and people post job listings here: part time, short term, freelance or project work, with pay shown as an amount per hour, day, shift or project.

  • Look: open a listing, read the details, and press the apply button with a short note.
  • Job alerts: choose a category and a province or city. When a matching listing appears you get a notification.
  • Post a listing: open the new job form from the Jobs tab. Choose the type, pay, city and whether remote work is possible.

8. Messages

  • First contact: if a person has not opened messages to you yet, you send one short request (Say hello). They can accept or say not now. You can send only a few requests a day.
  • Chats: after a quote is accepted, or a request is accepted, you have a normal conversation. Files and photos can be sent.
  • Group chats: start one from your people or a project. Activities and companies have their own group chat.
  • Block: on a provider page, press Block this person. They cannot message you. Unblock in Settings, under Blocked people.
  • Report: use the report link at the bottom of a post, message or profile to tell the team about scams or abuse.

9. Volunteer activities

  1. On the Post screen choose Volunteer, or open Activities.
  2. Pick a province in the drop-down and press Show.
  3. Open an activity and press Join. If it is full you go on the waiting list and are moved up automatically when a place opens.
  4. You get a reminder before it starts and a group chat with the other volunteers.
Organising: to create an activity you need a checked identity (section 11). Set the title, place, time and number of people.

11. The identity check

A green Identity checked mark tells others a person has shown a real ID to the team. It is needed to organise activities and to receive a payout account approval.

  1. Open Profile, then Identity check.
  2. Step 1: type your legal name, ID type (National ID card, Passport or Other), ID number and date of birth exactly as on the ID.
  3. Step 2: take a photo of the front of the ID. Make sure the text is readable and the whole card is in the frame.
  4. Step 3: take a selfie holding the ID next to your face.
  5. Press Submit for review. A person checks it within two working days and you get a notification.
Only Piron Work staff see the photos and details. They are never shown on your profile.

12. Companies and teams

A company has its own page, team, documents, shifts and tasks. Create one from Profile → My companies → Add company.

RoleCan do
OwnerEverything, and the only one who can publish a document or turn the public page on or off.
ManagerInvite people, schedule shifts, assign tasks, set pay terms, upload documents.
MemberSee their own shifts, tasks and pay terms, clock in and out, mark their tasks done.

Inside a company

  • Team: invite people by their profile. They accept from their notifications. You can show “Works at” on a profile if the person agrees.
  • Documents: upload files and give each one a level: Private (managers only), Team (everyone in the company) or Public (on the company page). Only the owner can make a document public. Visitors never see Private or Team files.
  • Public page: a page anyone can open with your open projects, jobs, activities and public documents. The owner switches it on.
  • Schedule: managers add a shift for a person with a date and time. Members press Clock in from 30 minutes before the shift until it ends, and Clock out when they finish.
  • Tasks: managers create a task, optionally assign it and set a due date. The assigned member moves it to Doing and Done.
  • Pay terms: a manager sets job title, duties and rate (per hour, day or month) for each person and keeps simple pay records. Members see only their own.

13. Payments tab

Everything about money is in one place. Remember: records only, no money moves through Piron Work, and these are not tax invoices.

PartWhat it does
To receive / To payTotals of your open invoices.
Create an invoiceLists your accepted jobs. Choose one and set the amount. An invoice cannot be more than the agreed price of the job.
Invoices and paymentsInvoices you issued and invoices sent to you.
Payout accountWhere you want to be paid: ABA, Wing or another bank, with the name and number. The team checks that the name matches your ID.
Team pay terms and recordsYour rate and hours for each company you belong to.

How an invoice moves

  1. The provider creates the invoice and shares it with the customer. It can be printed or saved as PDF.
  2. The customer pays the provider directly, then presses I have paid and chooses how and when.
  3. The provider answers Did the payment arrive? with Yes, payment received or Not received. If the customer paid in person and did not mark it, the provider can use Record a payment received.
  4. The invoice becomes Received. The provider can press Cancel this invoice while it is not paid.

14. Notifications and settings

Open Menu → Settings.

  • Notifications on your phone: switch phone notifications on for this device.
  • Email notifications: a quiet backup. You get an email only when a quote is accepted or work is delivered.
  • What to notify me about: switch off any of these groups: Projects and quotes, Messages, Activities, Reviews, Team work, Invoices and pay. Identity results, disputes and the job alerts you set up always arrive.
  • Reminders: a nudge for quotes waiting, work to confirm, deadlines, availability ending and activities tomorrow.
  • Show my work record: choose whether confirmed jobs appear on your profile.
  • Language and Blocked people are here too.

15. Staying safe

  • Look for the green mark. Identity checked means staff saw a real ID. Not having it does not mean a scam, but ask more questions.
  • Agree the price in writing inside the job chat before work starts, and pay only as agreed.
  • Pay the provider directly. Piron Work staff will never ask you to send money to them or share a password or code.
  • Meet in a public place for the first meeting, and tell someone where you are going.
  • Report and block. If someone scams, threatens or spams, report the post or person and block them. The team reviews reports.
  • Paused accounts. The team can pause an account that breaks the rules. A paused person can log in but cannot post, quote or message.
  • Your data. Read the Privacy and Terms pages linked at the bottom of the website. ID photos are kept private.
