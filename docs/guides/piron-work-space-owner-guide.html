1. What the Space is

Piron Work Space is a private area for you, the owner. It answers five questions:

QuestionSection
How many people use Piron Work and what is new?Overview, People
Is the marketplace actually working?Marketplace
What is broken right now?Overview status, Errors
Which new feature is hidden, and who can see it?Features
How much came in and went out?Money, Reports
Only the owner account can open it. Any other person, even another admin, sees “Not allowed” and receives no numbers. Everything you do in it is written to the audit log.

2. How to open it

  1. Log in with the owner account. You land on a page with two cards: Piron Work (the app) and Piron Work Space (the control room). Choose the Space.
  2. Later, click the Piron Work logo at any time to come back to the same two cards.
  3. Inside the app, open the Menu (three lines) and choose Piron Work Space.
  4. Or go straight to app.piron.tech/admin/space.

Moving around

The side list has Overview, People, Marketplace, Errors, Features, Money, Reports and Audit log. On a phone the list scrolls sideways. At the top, Open Piron Work goes back to the app and Requests and applicants opens the older admin page.

The Space is in English only.

4. The queues

These are the things people are waiting for you to decide. Open each from the tile on the Overview.

QueueWhat to do
Identity checksOpen the request: you see the written details (legal name, ID type, ID number, date of birth, machine-readable line if given) and two photos (the ID and the selfie holding it). Check the details match the photo and the face matches the ID. Choose checked or rejected and write a short note. The person is told within two working days.
ReportsRead what was reported and open the post or person. Set it to Reviewing, Resolved or Dismissed and add a note. If it is a scam or abuse, also pause the person (section 5).
DisputesRead both sides on the job. Contact them if you need more. Set Reviewing, then Resolved or Dismissed, with a resolution note both people can understand.
Payout accountsCompare the account holder name with the person’s checked ID name. Approve only when they match; otherwise reject with a reason. A person can use a payout account as verified only if their identity is checked and the account is approved. Any change by the person sends it back for review.
Project requests and applicantsRequests from the public form and providers who applied to join. Set a status, add a note, and match a request to a provider when you can help.
Every decision you press is written to the audit log with your name and the time.

5. People

The page

  • Stat cards: people, new this week, new this month, providers, identity checked, signed in over 7 days.
  • 30-day chart of new people, and Where they are (cities).
  • Search by name, role or city, and a drop-down: Everyone, Providers, Identity checked, New this week, Paused. The list shows up to 60, newest first.

One person

Open any row to see: joined date, last signed in, posts, quotes sent, jobs as customer and as provider, confirmed jobs, reviews and stars, reports against them and reports they filed, companies, and their latest posts. A link opens their public profile.

You never see email, phone, address or ID number here. Those stay private. Identity photos are in the Identity queue only.

Pause or resume an account

  1. Open the person. At the bottom, type a short reason and press Pause account.
  2. The person can still log in but sees “Your account is paused” and cannot post, quote, message or change anything. Their provider card and public record are hidden.
  3. To undo, open the same person and press Resume account. They must turn their provider switch on again themselves.
  • You cannot pause yourself or another admin.
  • Use it for scams and abuse. It is logged with your reason.
  • Limit: it stops the app. A technical person calling the database directly with their own token is not stopped. A full block needs a login ban, not set up yet.

7. Errors

When something breaks for a person, the app tells you here.

  • Grouping: the same error is one line with a count, where it happened (page, button or form, background call, phone), who it last hit and when.
  • Alert: if one error happens 5 times in 10 minutes you get a bell notification and an email. At most once an hour per error, and at most 3 alerts an hour in total.
  • Buttons: Fixed closes it (it reopens by itself if it happens again), Ignore hides noise, Reopen brings it back. Use the drop-down for Open, Fixed, Ignored or All.
  • Failed emails and push messages: the last failed calls with the address removed.
  • Reminder runs: the last 8 runs of the reminder job (every 30 minutes).
  • Kept for 60 days, then deleted automatically.
Check the alarm. Press Send a test error. You see the error screen, then a line “Test error from Piron Work Space” appears. Mark it Fixed. Do this after any big change.

If an error is a bug, copy its message and page and send them to your developer or to Claude together with what you pressed.

  2. For Chosen people, search and pick a person under the feature and press Add. Press Remove next to a name to take them out.
  3. A change takes effect on the next page the person opens.
  4. The Change log at the bottom shows who changed what and when.

9. Money

This is your own book: what comes in and what goes out for Piron Work. Piron Work does not touch users’ money.

Reading the page

  • Choose a month in the drop-down (the last 12).
  • Revenue, Costs and Profit (red if negative) for that month.
  • Paid between users: invoices people marked as received that month. It shows the size of the market. It is not your income.
  • Last 6 months table with a total row.

Adding an entry

  1. Choose the month at the top.
  2. Pick a revenue type (Service fee, Subscription, Paid listing, Advertising, Other income) and a cost type (Cloudflare, Supabase, Email, Domain names, Phone and internet, Legal and accounting, Marketing, Other cost).
  3. Type the amount in US dollars and an optional note.
  4. Tick Repeats every month for things that renew, such as hosting.
  5. Press Add as revenue or Add as cost. It uses the matching type.
  • Monthly entries count in that month and every month after, until you press Stop. Earlier months keep them.
  • One-off entries have a Delete button. Deleting is logged.
  • Download CSV (12 months) gives every entry that counts, month by month, for your accountant.
This is a simple book, not an accounting system or tax advice. Keep receipts and ask your accountant how to report income.

11. Audit log

Every admin action in one list, newest first, with who did it and when. Nobody can edit or delete a line.

  • Identity checks, reports, disputes and payout accounts decided.
  • Accounts paused or resumed (with your reason).
  • Feature switches and people added to a feature.
  • Money entries added, stopped or deleted.
  • Errors marked fixed, ignored or reopened.

Use the drop-down to show one kind. The last 200 are shown.

13. Safety and limits

  • Protect the owner login. Use a strong password you do not use anywhere else. Log out on shared computers. Anyone with this login sees everything above.
  • Owner only is enforced by the database, not only by hiding buttons. Other accounts get nothing back.
  • Private details stay private. Emails, phone numbers, addresses and ID numbers are not shown or exported in the Space.
  • Secrets (email key, push keys) live in Cloudflare and Supabase, never in the app pages. Never paste them into chat or documents.
  • Pause is not a login ban. See section 5.
  • Payments through Piron Work are locked. Holding or moving users’ money needs legal advice on National Bank of Cambodia licensing first. Nothing in the Space turns it on.
  • English only. The Space screens have no Khmer yet.
